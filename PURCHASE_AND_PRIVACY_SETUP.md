# PURCHASE AND PRIVACY SETUP

## Genuine product purchases

FlowGate Plus and hosted FlowGate licenses are genuine digital products or
services. They may be processed through an approved FlowGate merchant account
using an accurate, recognizable descriptor such as:

`FLOWGATE DIGITAL`

The processor, acquiring bank, and card network must approve the business
activity, descriptor, and merchant category.

## Crypto purchases stay crypto purchases

Buying USDC, XCN, XRP, or another asset through an on-ramp is a crypto purchase.
The regulated provider controls its merchant identity, classification,
statement presentation, KYC, fraud screening, pricing, refunds, and delivery.

Do not describe a crypto purchase as unrelated software, retail goods,
consulting, or another product.

## Privacy mode

Privacy mode can:

- Mask selected amounts and asset details on the webpage
- Erase locally saved receipts and interface preferences
- Clear receipts when the page closes, if enabled
- Operate without advertising or analytics trackers

It cannot:

- Hide the charge from a card issuer
- Change a merchant category code
- Remove provider or KYC records
- Alter blockchain history
- Avoid tax or reporting obligations
- Make a transaction anonymous

Use two visibly separate checkouts:

1. Purchase FlowGate Plus — a genuine digital-software purchase.
2. Buy crypto through provider — a provider-controlled crypto purchase.
