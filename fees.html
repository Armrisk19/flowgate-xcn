Fee Disclosure

Key rule: FlowGate does not use a hidden spread. Any FlowGate fee must be displayed separately or incorporated into a provider quote that clearly identifies the total price and amount received before confirmation.

Merchant description and classification

A FlowGate digital-product purchase should use an accurate and recognizable FlowGate descriptor when supported by the merchant processor. Crypto purchases are labeled and classified by the on-ramp provider. FlowGate does not promise that a crypto transaction will appear as an ordinary retail purchase.

FlowGate charges

FlowGate may earn a developer fee on completed crypto-to-crypto swaps when a thirdweb project is connected and the fee is activated in that project’s dashboard. The public website currently describes a planned 1.25% developer fee. The actual configured fee must match the public disclosure.

FlowGate Plus is a separate digital-service purchase. It does not include cryptocurrency and does not guarantee that any on-ramp, swap, bridge, or investment will succeed.

Third-party charges

Card providers, exchanges, liquidity providers, thirdweb, blockchain networks, and bridges may charge their own fees or include market spread. Those amounts vary and are controlled by the live quote or transaction.

Future partner fee

FlowGate may activate an additional on-ramp partner fee only after a regulated provider approves the business and contractually supports that fee. It must be shown before payment.

Contact

The operator must publish a valid support email and legal business information before broad public launch.
