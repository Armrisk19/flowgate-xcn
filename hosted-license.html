Hosted License Framework

Important: This is a commercial template for review by counsel, not a signed agreement.

Hosted service—not source-code sale

The customer receives access to a hosted, branded FlowGate portal during an active subscription. Ownership of the FlowGate core code, operator project, partner integrations, attribution system, and master platform configuration remains with the operator unless a separate written acquisition agreement states otherwise.

Operator attribution

The hosted portal may retain visible “Powered by FlowGate” branding, the operator’s thirdweb project, approved partner identifiers, campaign tracking, and disclosed transaction-fee configuration. The customer agrees not to remove, bypass, conceal, or interfere with those elements.

Recurring charges

The customer pays the written setup fee and monthly platform fee. Service may be suspended after failed payment, material misuse, provider termination, legal restrictions, or breach of the agreement.

No lifetime promise

Referral programs, partner fees, payment methods, supported assets, provider availability, and commission terms may change or end. Neither party guarantees perpetual revenue, uninterrupted service, user volume, conversion rates, or earnings.

Provider responsibility

Payment providers and exchanges control KYC, fraud review, card acceptance, transaction execution, refunds, chargebacks, asset support, and geographic eligibility. The hosted customer must not describe itself as the provider, custodian, broker, or exchange unless separately authorized.

Cancellation and transition

The final agreement should state notice periods, data export, domain handling, outstanding balances, customer support obligations, and what happens to the branded portal after cancellation.

Contact

Add a valid support email and legal business information before offering hosted licenses.
