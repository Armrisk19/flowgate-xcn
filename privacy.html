Privacy Policy

FlowGate is designed to minimize data collection. The card provider—not FlowGate—collects card and identity information during provider checkout.

Privacy mode

Privacy mode masks selected information on the webpage and can erase locally stored receipts. It does not erase provider records, card statements, blockchain history, compliance records, or information held by banks and payment processors.

Local device data

FlowGate may store interface preferences, toolkit unlock status, and receipts in the browser’s local storage. This data normally stays on the user’s device unless the user exports or shares it.

Third-party widgets

Embedded providers may collect wallet, device, payment, identity, transaction, and compliance information under their own privacy policies. FlowGate does not control those practices.

Analytics

Do not add analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking without updating this policy and implementing any required consent controls.

Security

FlowGate never asks users to provide a seed phrase or private key. Users should never enter those secrets into FlowGate or any linked service.

Contact

The operator must publish a valid support email and legal business information before broad public launch.
