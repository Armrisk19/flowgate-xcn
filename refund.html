Refund Policy

Crypto purchases, swaps, bridges, gas fees, and blockchain transfers are controlled by third parties and may be irreversible. FlowGate cannot reverse or refund those transactions.

Provider transactions

Questions about a card purchase, declined payment, identity review, charge, or crypto delivery must be directed to the provider shown in the checkout receipt.

FlowGate Plus

FlowGate Plus is a digital service delivered immediately after successful payment. Refund eligibility, if offered, must be stated by the operator’s final business policy and comply with applicable law. Chargebacks or disputes do not reverse blockchain transactions.

White-label services

White-label deposits and refunds must be governed by a separate written scope, milestone, delivery, and cancellation agreement. Do not accept payment before that agreement is complete.
