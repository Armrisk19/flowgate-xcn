Crypto Risk Disclosure

Cryptocurrency purchases and blockchain transactions involve substantial risk. Only transact with funds you can afford to lose.

Price and liquidity

Crypto prices can move rapidly. Quoted amounts may change, routes may fail, liquidity may be limited, and price impact can be significant.

Network and smart-contract risk

Blockchain congestion, gas costs, bridge failures, contract vulnerabilities, wallet compromise, phishing, and irreversible transactions can cause loss.

Transaction visibility

Privacy mode does not make a transaction anonymous or remove it from bank, provider, blockchain, tax, or compliance records.

Credit-card risk

A card issuer may decline a crypto transaction or treat it differently from a normal retail purchase. Users should check their card agreement, fees, interest treatment, and ability to repay before using credit.

Regulatory and provider risk

Eligibility, KYC requirements, supported assets, locations, payment methods, and provider availability can change without notice.

Contact

The operator must publish a valid support email and legal business information before broad public launch.
